# FlightGlance Privacy Policy

_Effective 5 October 2026_

FlightGlance is an iPhone app that shows your progress during a flight using your iPhone's GPS.
It is published by Tony Weston ("I", "me"). This policy explains what the app does with your
information. The short version: **nothing leaves your iPhone.**

## What FlightGlance collects

**Nothing.** FlightGlance does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. It has no
account, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting service and no tracking of
any kind. It does not use the internet in flight, and the map is built into the app.

## Information used on your iPhone

To work, the app uses some information **only on your device**:

- **Location.** With your permission, FlightGlance reads your iPhone's GPS to show where you are,
  your ground speed, GPS altitude and course, and to estimate your arrival time. If you leave
  "Show on Lock Screen" switched on, it continues to read your location while your iPhone is
  locked, every few minutes, to keep the Lock Screen display up to date. iOS shows a location
  indicator while this happens.
- **Your flight.** The airports you choose and the GPS track recorded during the flight are saved
  in the app's private storage on your iPhone so the flight can resume if the app is closed.
  They are deleted when you tap **End Flight**, or when you delete the app.
- **Settings.** Your choices such as units and map style are stored on your iPhone.

None of this is sent to me or to anyone else. I have no servers and no way to see it.

## Permissions

You can change or withdraw location permission at any time in **Settings → FlightGlance →
Location**. You can turn off the Lock Screen display in the app, or in **Settings →
FlightGlance → Live Activities**. Without location permission the app cannot show your position,
but it will not stop working in any other way.

## Backups

If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or a computer, the app's saved flight may be included in that
backup, as with any app's data. Backups are handled by Apple and your device settings, not by
FlightGlance.

## Children

FlightGlance does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new
effective date. Because the app collects no data, any change would describe a change in what the
app does before it happens.
