# FlightGlance Support

Need help, found a bug, or have an idea?
**[Open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/tonywestonuk/flightglance/issues)** and I'll get
back to you.

## Common questions

**It says "Searching for GPS" and never finds me.**
GPS needs a view of the sky. Inside an aircraft, a window seat works best; hold the phone near the
window for a minute. Make sure FlightGlance is allowed to use your location
(**Settings → FlightGlance → Location → While Using the App**) and that **Precise Location** is on.

**Does it work in Airplane Mode?**
Yes. GPS is a receiver and keeps working in Airplane Mode, and the map is built into the app. No
Wi-Fi or data is needed.

**Nothing appears on my Lock Screen.**
Check that **Show on Lock Screen** is on in the app, and that Live Activities are allowed in
**Settings → FlightGlance → Live Activities**. iOS ends a Live Activity after 8 hours; on longer
flights, open the app to start a new one.

**My altitude is different from what the crew announced.**
FlightGlance shows GPS altitude above sea level. Aircraft altimeters use air pressure, so the two
can differ by several hundred feet.

**The arrival time is earlier than the airline's.**
The estimate uses the straight-line distance to your destination and your recent ground speed. It
can't know about the approach, holding or taxiing, so it tends to be a little early.

**There's no speed or arrival time on the ground.**
Speed-based readings appear once you're moving faster than about 50 knots, so they show up after
take-off.

**Is my location shared with anyone?**
No. Your location and flight track stay on your iPhone and are deleted when you end the flight.
See the [privacy policy](PRIVACY.md).
